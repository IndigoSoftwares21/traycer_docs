---
title: "Get transfer"
description: "Look up one indexed transaction by its on-chain hash."
openapi: "GET /transfers/{txHash}"
---

<Note>
  Requires a `read` scope key.
</Note>

Returns the indexed transaction with that hash, scoped to your organization. Despite the name, it resolves deposits as well as withdrawals — it is a lookup by hash over the same ledger [`GET /transactions`](/api-reference/transactions/list-transactions) pages through.

## A fresh broadcast is not immediately findable

<Warning>
  A `txId` returned by [Send a transfer](/api-reference/transfers/send-transfer) is not queryable here until the poller has indexed the containing block. Until then you get:

  ```json
  {
    "message": "Validation failed",
    "code": 400,
    "validationErrors": [
      { "path": ["txHash"], "message": "Transaction not found or does not belong to this organization" }
    ]
  }
  ```

  That is expected. It is **not** evidence that the transfer failed.
</Warning>

The poller runs every 10 seconds and stays 3 confirmations behind the chain head, so expect a delay of at least that.

<Tip>
  Do not poll this in a tight loop after broadcasting. Record the hash as pending in your own system, let the `transaction.withdrawal` webhook flip it to confirmed, and use a slower reconciliation sweep over `GET /transactions` as the backstop. See [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks).
</Tip>

## txId means something different here

<Warning>
  In the **send** response, `txId` is the on-chain hash. In **this** response, `txId` is Traycer's internal row identifier and the on-chain hash is `txHash`.

  Match a send's `txId` against this endpoint's `txHash`.
</Warning>

## Indexing coverage

<Note>
  **Currently Tron only.** Transactions on Ethereum, BSC, and Bitcoin are not indexed, so this endpoint will never resolve their hashes even when the transfer succeeded on-chain.
</Note>
